# Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

PaletteCraft is a free, open-source app for painters. It does not collect,
send or sell any personal data; this website only counts visits, without cookies. This page explains what stays where.

## Your pictures

Pictures you open are read and analysed on your own device, by the app itself. They are
never uploaded anywhere: not in the desktop app, and not in the web app, which runs
entirely in your browser.

## Your palettes and settings

- **Desktop app:** your palettes and the list of recently opened pictures (with each
  picture's settings) are saved as files in your user settings folder, in a folder named
  `PaletteCraft`.
- **Web app:** your palettes and the list of recently opened pictures (with each picture's
  settings) are saved in your browser's storage on your device. As a browser cannot open a
  file again by itself, the web app also keeps a copy of the last 10 pictures you opened
  there, so you can open them from the list again; *Clear recent list* deletes them. The
  app's own files are kept by the browser so it can start without a network connection.

You can delete this data at any time: remove the settings folder (desktop), or clear the
site's data in your browser's settings (web app).

## PDF brochures

*Export PDF* creates the brochure on your device. The desktop app saves it where you
choose; the web app hands it to your browser as a download.

## No accounts, no tracking

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
code, sets no cookies, and makes no network requests of its own, except the update check
below. This applies to the desktop app and to the web app.

## Visit counts on this website

The pages of this website (not the web app) count visits with
[GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), a small open-source statistics service, to
show how many people find the project. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across
sites. For each page view it receives the page address, the page you came from, your
browser and screen size, and the country worked out from your IP address; your IP
address itself is not stored. See
[GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).
This website loads no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

## Checking for a newer version

Only when you click the app's name (on a phone, its logo), the app asks GitHub for the
number of the latest release, at `api.github.com`. Nothing about you, your pictures or your
palettes is sent with it; GitHub receives what any web server receives, such as your IP
address (see below). The app never checks on its own.

## Services this project uses

- **GitHub Pages** hosts this website and the web app, and **GitHub** hosts the source
  code and the downloads. When you visit, GitHub receives the usual information any web
  server receives, such as your IP address; see the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Ko-fi.** The *Support on Ko-fi* button only opens Ko-fi's website in your browser.
  Nothing is sent to Ko-fi unless you choose to leave a tip there; Ko-fi's own privacy
  policy, linked at the bottom of [ko-fi.com](https://ko-fi.com), then applies.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date,
and the history of changes is kept in the
[source repository](https://github.com/skrilic/PaletteCraft/commits/main/PRIVACY.md).
For questions, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/skrilic/PaletteCraft/issues).
